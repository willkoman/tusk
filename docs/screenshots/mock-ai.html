Which customers churned last quarter? Revenue excludes refunds per our skill.

Customers whose last non-refunded order is older than 90 days, joined on the declared FK orders.user_id → users.id:

SELECT u.email, max(o.created_at) AS last_order FROM users u JOIN orders o ON o.user_id = u.id AND o.status <> 'refunded' GROUP BY 1 HAVING max(o.created_at) < now() - interval '90 days' ORDER BY last_order;
